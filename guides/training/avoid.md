# Training lanes to avoid

Lanes that bill a card with no verified stop, and every page read for the training guides. Every figure is from the vendor's own page, read on 2026-11-23, unless a cell says otherwise. The rule is in [Training](../training.md).

| Lane | Why |
| --- | --- |
| [Tinker](https://thinkingmachines.ai/tinker/pricing/) | Per-token billing for LoRA training, from $0.396 per million tokens for GPT-OSS-20B to $32.00 for Kimi-K3.1-Pro. The pages read name no free credit, no card rule and no data terms ([Tinker models](https://tinker-docs.thinkingmachines.ai/tinker/models/), read 2026-11-23). |
| Modal Model APIs, and Modal past its credit | Model APIs are not covered by the plan credit. They bill from the first request ([Modal pricing](https://modal.com/pricing), read 2026-11-23). See [Modal](../modal.md). |
| Hugging Face prepaid credit and Colab Pay As You Go | Both turn a capped plan into a metered one. Past quota, ZeroGPU costs $1 per 10 minutes from prepaid credit ([ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu), read 2026-11-23). |
| Google Cloud's free trial | Per Google's page, $300 for 90 days. A card is required, and you cannot add GPUs to VM instances while on the trial ([Free cloud features](https://docs.cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features), read 2026-11-23). A budget that disables billing is not a cap: "Following the steps in this example doesn't guarantee that you won't spend more than your budget". It also shuts down every resource in the project ([Disable billing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/disable-billing-with-notifications), read 2026-11-23). |
| Any lane with a card on file and no verified stop | AMD bills once a card is attached and the credit is gone. Detach the card or set the limit first. |
| Kaggle for commercial work | Its terms limit use to non-commercial work. |
